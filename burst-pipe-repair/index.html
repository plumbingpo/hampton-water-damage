Few water damage events are as common, or as potentially devastating to a property, as a pipe that bursts. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

When a pipe bursts in Hampton or the surrounding area, Hampton Restoration runs an emergency response around the clock. Our restoration technicians can help you locate and shut off your water supply, and we coordinate with licensed plumbers so the plumbing repair is done properly. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

Structural drying comes next and covers the wet drywall, the framing, the insulation, the subfloor and the finished flooring above it. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Leak detectors placed close to main supply lines and the water heater give early warning
  • Never shut the furnace off completely during a winter trip, or pipes inside may freeze
  • Monitor your water pressure, because excessive pressure (above 80 PSI) strains pipes and fittings
  • Have a licensed plumber inspect your plumbing system every few years for wear and corrosion
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
  • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor faucets before the first freeze

The Benefits of Professional Burst Pipe Repair

The damage from a burst pipe tends to reach well past the wet areas in plain view. It moves between ceiling joists, inside floor assemblies, beside electrical conduit and down wall cavities, soaking materials you might not find for days or even weeks. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the water's path and pinpoint every affected area right away.

The machines we use for extraction and drying are in another class from anything sold for home use. Compared with a shop vacuum, truck-mounted extraction is hundreds of times faster, and compared with a residential dehumidifier, a commercial one removes 10 to 20 times as much moisture. Mold can begin growing in 24 to 48 hours, so this kind of power and speed matters a great deal.

Signs You Need Burst Pipe Repair in Hampton

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • No water at a tap in freezing weather, a sign the line is frozen
  • A stained, soggy or bowed section of drywall overhead or beside you
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • A hidden leak often shows up first as a much larger water bill
  • Hissing or rushing noises in the walls with every tap turned off
  • Pipes coated in ice or frost when temperatures drop
